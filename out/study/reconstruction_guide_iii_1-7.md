# Reconstruction: What Kaspi Would Say on Guide III:1–7

*An editorial reconstruction — not a recovered text.* The 1848 print has no
commentary on the Merkabah chapters: page 121 runs from a three-line פרק א
straight to פרק ז, because Kaspi honoured the rule he quotes there —
`שלא ילומד אלא פנים בפנים`. But he did not stay silent elsewhere. This file
assembles every surviving statement of his that bears on Maimonides' model
in III:1–7, and reconstructs, claim by claim, the position he would take —
each claim tagged **[A]** attested in his own words, **[I⁺]** strongly
inferred from attested doctrine, or **[I⁻]** conjectural. The direct
treatment exists: **מנורת כסף** (עשרה כלי כסף II, pp. 75–142, not yet in
hand). Section 6 states predictions this reconstruction makes about it —
when the pages arrive, they will judge this file.

Citations: `AK/MK part:chapter` = the project's study pack (established
text); `raw:volume p.N` = the raw library (unarbitrated; verify against the
scan).

---

## 1. The epistemic frame: the model is a reading, not a demonstration [A]

Kaspi's one extended statement on the Guide's Merkabah exposition is his
Maskiyot comment on the Part III preface (MK 3:intro), and it fixes his
stance precisely. Hinting in writing is lawful — `ואמנם הבאור וג', ר״ל אבל
הרמז מותר` — and Maimonides' identification of the vision's referents is,
for Kaspi, well-grounded opinion and not proof:

> שהענין כן בלא ספק ♦ ר״ל לפי הנראה לי ממה שהורני, אבל ההוראה ההיא אינו
> מופת הכרחי, ולכן אפשר שיהיה הענין חלופו. (MK 3:intro)

So on the *model* of III:1–7 he would say first: it is the best available
decoding, it is not necessary, and a philosopher must keep the modal
difference in view. This is Kaspi the logician applying his standing
doctrine that prophetic parable "sustains many ways, all of them sound" —
`הדבר הוא סובל דרכים רבים כלם נכוחים` (AK 1:4, on ראה/הביט/חזה) — to
Maimonides himself.

## 2. His signature widening: the Merkabah is not only Ezekiel [A]

Kaspi tells us what מנורת כסף argues, in his comment on Jacob's ladder:

> ואנחנו כבר יעדנו ס' מנורת כסף, זכרנו שם כי מעשה מרכבה יש בתורה בשלשה
> מקומות ושלשה בנביאים, ואחד מהם הוא ענין הסולם בתורה… ושם בארנו… וכי יש
> משל נמצא בספר בטלמיוס כי גנב זה מן התורה כמו שגנבו כל החכמות מספרי
> הקדש שלנו. (AK 1:15)

Three Merkabah loci in the Torah and three in the Prophets — the ladder
among them — against "the crowd" who find it only in Ezekiel (so too
raw:adnei_kesef_1, the Isaiah material: the complaint that people
`יחשבו שמעשה מרכבה… הוא לבדו נכון ביחזקאל`). On III:1–7 he would therefore
say: Maimonides expounds *one* instance of a recurring vision-type, and the
exposition must hold for Isaiah 6, Zechariah's visions, Sinai's `ויראו את
אלהי ישראל` (AK 1:4–5), and the ladder — he explicitly folds Zechariah's
standing angel and Ezekiel's `בעמדם יעמודו` into `כלל מעשה מרכבה` (AK 1:13,
1:38). The Ptolemy remark adds his characteristic frame: the Greeks carry a
stolen copy of the same parable, so pagan cosmology may be read back into
it without apology.

## 3. The cosmological identification he would endorse [A fragments, I⁺ joins]

The pieces he did print assemble into most of Maimonides' map:

- **The חיות are the spheres, and they are intellectual.** His entire
  comment on III:1 is one sentence doing exactly this: `פני אדם נטות וג',
  כי כולם בעלי שכל` (AK 3:1) — the four faces incline toward the human face
  because all the chayot are possessors of intellect, the living rational
  spheres of Guide II:5. **[A]**
- **Their motion is perpetual, their "standing" is endurance.** `בעמדם
  יעמודו — כלומר בקיומם יקויימו, כי לגלגלים אין מנוחה לתנועת ההעתק, וכל זה
  בכלל מעשה מרכבה` (AK 1:13). **[A]**
- **The throne stands above the celestial body.** On רם ונשא: Isaiah's
  throne `אין פירושו הגשם השמיימי רק הכסא שזכר יחזקאל שהוא למעלה מהגשם
  השמיימי, שענינו כהבדלה גדולה מעלה` (AK 1:20) — the kisse is the rank
  beyond the ninth sphere, the separate realm. **[A]**
- **The mover-hierarchy supplies the mechanics.** His Part II material
  gives the cascade he would deploy for רוח החיה באופנים: each sphere moved
  by its separate intellect, `כיחס כל שכל נבדל המיוחד בגלגל לגלגל ההוא`,
  with the Active Intellect standing to the sublunar elements as the
  nivdalim stand to their spheres (AK 2:4; AK 2:intro, the propositions).
  Hence the אופנים — bound to the chayot, moved with and by them — he
  would read as Maimonides does: the sublunar bodies/elements dragged in
  the spheres' train. **[I⁺]** — the identification of the ofanim is the
  one link he never states in the surviving prints.
- **The divine speech of the vision is mediated.** `כל מתחדש מאתו ית' יוחס
  לדברו… יפרוש המאמרות והדבורים המיוחסים לשם במעשה בראשית ומעשה מרכבה`
  (AK 1:23): the qol and dibbur of the vision are the emanated order, not
  audible words. **[A]**

## 4. The philological layer he would add [A]

Where Maimonides decodes referents, Kaspi habitually adds the grammarian's
stratum, and his one surviving III:7 fragment is exactly that: the vision's
dates — `ובחדש וביום… ברביעי ובששי, הוא רומז חדש רביעי וששי, ומניסן מנינן,
ואמנם גם אנו רואים בס' יחזקאל שכן הוא נוהג בהרבה נבואות` (AK 3:7) — read
with calendar realism and paralleled across the prophetic books. Expect,
in any fuller treatment: the Chagigah dicta he already invokes at the
part's head (`והבן שיעור מה שהיישרו אליו… שגילן עתיק יומיא`, MK 3:intro),
the Isaiah-versus-Ezekiel comparison in Chagigah's terms, and root-level
analysis of חשמל, רקיע, נגה as שיתוף cases — the method his Part I
chapters apply to ראה, עמד, רם, יצא, רגל, הלך one by one. **[A** for the
method; **I⁺** for its application to these words.]

## 5. What he would *not* say [I⁺]

No kabbalistic hypostases — his Merkabah is Aristotelian cosmology seen in
prophetic imagination; no claim of demonstration (§1); and no confinement
of the mystery to Ezekiel (§2). And, on his own rule, no full disclosure:
the deepest identification (the דמות כמראה אדם of III:7's summit) he would
gesture toward the נבדלים and the First Cause and then stop, as Maimonides
stops — `ולא תוחיל לשמוע ממני בזה דבר` is a restraint Kaspi quotes and
keeps (AK 3:7 end; his reserved אוצר ה׳ was the announced address for
more).

## 6. Predictions this reconstruction makes about מנורת כסף (pp. 75–142)

Falsifiable when the pages arrive:

1. It enumerates six Merkabah loci — three Torah (the ladder among them),
   three Prophets — and treats them as one vision-type (per AK 1:15).
2. It identifies the chayot with the intelligent spheres and reads the
   faces through the primacy of the human/intellectual face (per AK 3:1).
3. It places the throne above the ninth sphere as the separate realm (per
   AK 1:20).
4. It carries a Ptolemy (or general Greek-theft) comparison (per AK 1:15).
5. It marks Maimonides' decoding as strong but non-demonstrative,
   `אפשר שיהיה הענין חלופו` (per MK 3:intro).

Wrong on two or more, and this file should be rewritten in shame and with
gratitude.
